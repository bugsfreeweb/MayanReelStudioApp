Why this exists

Most editors assume you upload your footage.

This one assumes the opposite — nothing leaves the machine, nothing extra to install, and every AI step is a replaceable artifact you can regenerate on its own.

Nothing leaves

No uploads, no account, no telemetry. Decode, effects, render and encode all run locally.

Nothing to install

ffmpeg, hardware encoders, the VST3 host and 48 Bengali families ship inside the installer.

Free & permissive

MIT licence, permissive dependencies only — no watermark, no subscription, no pay-per-render.

Bengali first

Fonts, subtitles, layout and voice treat বাংলা as first-class, never a translation layer.

What you can do

One studio. Every stage of the cut.

A frame-accurate editor, a 3D scene designer, a subtitle and audio workstation and a story-to-video pipeline — in one app.

Edit picture

Multi-track timeline with frame-accurate scrub.

  • Ripple delete, slip, slide, nesting
  • Transitions, blend modes, masks, 3D transform
  • Speed ramps 0.25×–4×, pitch-corrected
  • Colour: curves, HSL, scopes, LUTs, HDR
  • Multicam sync · every social canvas

Sound

Mix, repair and score on the timeline.

  • EQ, compression, gate, limiter, denoise
  • VST3 plugin host, per-clip inserts
  • Beat detection & waveform preview
  • Voiceover recorded straight to the timeline

Titles & captions

Text, subtitles and graphics that stay editable.

  • Animated text: fills, outlines, gradients
  • Automatic captions + full subtitle editor
  • 48 Bengali families · import any font
  • SRT / VTT in and out, burn-in or sidecar

3D & motion

Build scenes, light them, render into the edit.

  • Motion Creator: primitives, materials, textures
  • Animate cameras & objects, render frames
  • Optional Blender rigging bridge
agent

AI that edits with you

An agent that drives the editor through tools.

  • Add tracks, cut, grade, caption, render
  • Your own OpenAI-compatible key or local model
  • Every action is an undoable edit

Export

Deliverables that look the same as the preview.

  • H.264 / H.265 / ProRes / WebM
  • Hardware encoders: nvenc → qsv → amf → libx264
  • Optional AI upscaling · HDR · broadcast-legal
